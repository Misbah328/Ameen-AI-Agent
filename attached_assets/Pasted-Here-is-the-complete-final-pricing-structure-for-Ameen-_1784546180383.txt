Here is the complete, final pricing structure for Ameen Secretary. This consolidates and replaces all earlier pricing messages — please use this as the single source of truth for the pricing page and account provisioning logic.

TIER 1 — AMEEN BASIC — 1,500 SAR/month

Target: a single board with no sub-committees, doing the legal minimum in governance.

Limits:
* 1 board only, no sub-committees
* Up to 5 members
* 4 meetings per YEAR (matches the legal minimum board meeting requirement under Saudi Companies Law)
* 1 entity (no subsidiaries)
* 30 "Ask Ameen" queries/month
* 1 GB storage

Included:
* Live recording / file upload / paste text
* Live transcription
* Basic task/decision extraction (view-only, no interactive tracking)
* One-time free Governance Maturity Assessment
* 3 of 10 document types (Minutes AR, Minutes EN, Bilingual Minutes)

Not included: e-signature, task follow-up board, sub-committees, Circular Resolutions.

---

TIER 2 — AMEEN PLUS — 4,500 SAR/month

Target: a mid-size company wanting a full, legally valid approval cycle, without needing daily execution tracking yet.

Limits:
* Up to 3 boards/committees
* Up to 20 members
* 30 meetings per YEAR
* 1 entity
* 200 "Ask Ameen" queries/month
* 10 GB storage

Included (everything in Basic, plus):
* Full 4-stage minutes approval cycle (Draft -> Review -> Approval -> Archived)
* Item-level comments visible to all attendees, with dual accept/reject authority (Secretary + Chairman)
* Full e-signature support (digital AND manual/wet signature with scanned upload)
* Unique reference code for every archived minutes record
* All 10 document generation types
* Expanded "Ask Ameen" usage

Locked (visible upgrade prompt, not hidden): interactive Follow-Up & Execution board — tasks appear but status can't be updated, no proactive check-ins. Sub-committee management and Circular Resolutions are also not available.

Overage pricing beyond 30 meetings/year:
* Meetings 31–40: 400 SAR each
* Meetings 41–50: 600 SAR each
* Beyond 50: no further overage — upgrade to Advanced required to continue

---

TIER 3 — AMEEN ADVANCED — 7,000 SAR/month (marked "Recommended")

Target: a board or company ready to run the platform at full operational capacity.

Limits:
* Unlimited boards/committees
* Up to 50 members
* Effectively unlimited meetings (soft abuse-prevention cap at 150/year)
* 1 entity
* 1,000 "Ask Ameen" queries/month
* 100 GB storage

Included (everything in Plus, plus):
* Full interactive Follow-Up & Execution: status updates, automatic escalation, scheduled proactive check-ins with task owners, periodic status reports for managers, Pre-Meeting Smart Report
* Encrypted member-to-member messaging
* Sub-committee management with independent cycles
* Circular Resolutions (full workflow: drafting, voting, quorum, signature)
* "Resolution" formal formatting tag + standalone resolution export
* Effectively unlimited "Ask Ameen" usage

Overage pricing beyond 150 meetings/year: 100 SAR/meeting, uncapped (this ceiling exists purely to prevent abuse of a low-cost account for heavy-scale usage — genuine customers should never hit it).

---

TIER 4 — AMEEN ENTERPRISE — Custom pricing, sales-assisted only

Target: holding companies and multi-entity groups. No self-serve trial — lead form/contact request, followed by custom proposal and contract.

Included (everything in Advanced, plus):
* Multi-entity management (holding company + all subsidiaries, unified dashboard)
* Isolated/private deployment option
* Custom integrations (e.g. SAP)
* Dedicated account manager, onboarding, and training
* Compliance reporting for regulatory bodies (CMA, etc.)
* SLA agreement

No published price — show "Contact Sales" only.

---

UNIFIED TRIAL POLICY (Basic, Plus, Advanced only — Enterprise excluded)

* 3-day free trial for all three self-serve tiers, no exceptions.
* Payment card required at signup.
* Billing starts automatically when the trial ends unless canceled.
* Reminder (email + WhatsApp) 24 hours before trial ends and billing begins.

---

SMART OVERAGE UX

Before confirming any meeting that would trigger an overage charge, show the exact fee and a choice to continue or upgrade (e.g. "This is meeting #31 this year — a 400 SAR charge will be added. Continue, or upgrade to Advanced for unlimited meetings and full task follow-up?"). Also show a proactive banner once projected year-to-date overage charges exceed the cost difference to the next tier. No surprise billing — every charge is confirmed before the meeting is created.
