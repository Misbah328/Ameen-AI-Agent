STEP 1 — Creation
An authorized person (Secretary or authorized member) creates the resolution directly, without going through the "schedule a meeting" path:
* Title and short description of the subject
* Full text of the resolution to be voted on
* Supporting attachments (if any)
* Voting body — auto-pulled based on entity/committee type
* Fixed deadline for comments and voting

---

STEP 2 — Circulation to Members
Once marked "Ready to Circulate":
* Instant notification to every relevant member (email + WhatsApp + in-platform link)
* Each member sees their own individually watermarked copy
* The system logs who opened it and when (real tracking, not assumed)

---

STEP 3 — Comments (identical mechanism to minutes review)
On the resolution text itself, each member can:
* Comment on a specific clause
* Propose a specific correction
* Silently approve with no comment
All comments are visible to everyone (not private), tagged with commenter name, timestamp, and exact clause referenced.

---

STEP 4 — Resolving Comments
Same dual authority as the minutes cycle: both the Secretary AND the Chairman can accept or reject any comment, with the decision-maker and reasoning logged. Accepted comments apply automatically to the resolution text; rejected ones are logged with the reason.

---

STEP 5 — Formal Vote
After the comment deadline closes:
* Each member votes: Approve / Reject (mandatory reason) / Abstain
* Live counter shows progress (e.g. "5 of 7 voted — 4 approve")
* Quorum auto-calculated per entity/resolution type

---

STEP 6 — Outcome
* Quorum + majority reached → automatically approved
* Deadline passes without quorum → resolution lapses and is automatically suggested as an agenda item for the next Board meeting
* Explicit rejection → logged as rejected with documented reasons

---

STEP 7 — Signature
Every member who voted "Approve" signs officially — digital or manual/wet with scanned upload, identical mechanism to minutes signing, with a live tracking panel of who has/hasn't signed.

---

STEP 8 — Registration in the Decision Registry
Once signatures are complete, the resolution is automatically logged in the unified Decision Registry, tagged "Circular Resolution," with its own independent reference code (e.g. CR-2026-014). Any linked tasks activate on the Follow-Up & Execution board.

---

STEP 9 — Post-approval tracking status: "Recorded in Minutes" or not

After approval, give the Secretary (exclusively) the ability to update a SEPARATE status field on each Circular Resolution, independent from its approval status:

* "Not Yet Recorded" (default after approval)
* "Recorded in Minutes No. [X]" — with a direct link to that specific Board meeting's archived minutes by its reference code

Why this matters: governance practice often requires a resolution passed outside a live meeting to be formally noted/ratified in the minutes of the next official Board meeting. This field lets the Secretary track which Circular Resolutions still need that follow-up citation.

This should also proactively surface in the Pre-Meeting Smart Report (e.g. "3 Circular Resolutions are not yet recorded in any official minutes") so it doesn't get forgotten before the next meeting.

---

STEP 10 — MAKE THIS STATUS SEARCHABLE (new requirement)

The "Recorded in Minutes" / "Not Yet Recorded" status must be a filterable, searchable field wherever Circular Resolutions or the Decision Registry are browsed — not just visible on each individual resolution's page. Specifically:

* In the global search bar and the Decision Registry's filter panel, add "Recording Status" as a filter option (Recorded / Not Yet Recorded).
* Search results for a specific resolution should display its recording status directly in the result row/card (not require opening the resolution to see it).
* Support a one-click filtered view: "Show all Circular Resolutions not yet recorded in minutes" — this should be a saved/quick filter the Secretary can access in one click, since it's a recurring governance task, not just an occasional search.
* This status field should also be exportable/reportable (e.g. as part of an audit export) so a reviewer can verify at a glance which resolutions still lack formal minute citation.

---

FULL VISUAL FLOW (for reference)

Create resolution -> circulate to members (with open-tracking) -> item-level comments (visible to all) -> Secretary + Chairman resolve comments -> formal vote with quorum check -> approved or lapses -> signatures collected -> registered in Decision Registry (own reference code) -> tasks activated -> Secretary tracks "Recorded in Minutes" status (searchable/filterable everywhere resolutions appear) -> surfaced automatically in Pre-Meeting Report if still unrecorded.
