❓ FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does AI meeting transcription work?
▾
Ameen uses advanced AI models trained on Arabic and English to recognise speech and transcribe it. Upload a recording or connect live and get structured minutes in minutes.
Is our board data secure?
▾
Yes. All data is encrypted with AES-256 at rest and in transit. You have a full immutable audit trail for every action performed on every document.
Can multiple members sign simultaneously?
▾
Yes. Ameen supports parallel or sequential approval workflows. Members can sign from any device at any time, with instant notifications at each step.
What happens after the free trial ends?
▾
After 7 days you can choose a plan to continue. Your data is preserved. No credit card is required during the trial period.
Does Ameen integrate with Zoom or Microsoft Teams?
▾
Ameen supports uploading recordings from all platforms. Direct live integration with Zoom and Teams is on the roadmap and coming soon.
Is the platform fully available in Arabic?
▾
Yes. Ameen is built natively for Arabic with full RTL support, Tajawal font, and AI trained specifically on Arabic dialects and governance terminology.
